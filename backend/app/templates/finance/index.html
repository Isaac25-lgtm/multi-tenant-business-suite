How the finance section works
  1. Individual loans: add the borrower under Clients, then issue the loan under Individual Loans.
  2. Group loans: go straight to Group Loans and enter the group and its members.
  3. Open any loan to record a payment, download statements, or (managers) record discounts and reverse mistakes.
  4. Employees can enter today's and yesterday's transactions; managers can enter any date.
